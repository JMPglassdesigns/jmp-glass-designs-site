Process

Simple, clear, start to finish

  1. 1

    Consult & Measure

    We talk through the look, the hardware and how you’ll use the space, then take precise field measurements.

  2. 2

    Quote

    A clear, itemized quote covering glass, hardware and installation — no guesswork.

  3. 3

    Shop Drawings

    Dimensioned drawings for your review and sign-off before anything is ordered or cut.

  4. 4

    Fabrication

    Glass is cut, edged and tempered to the approved drawings, with hardware matched to your finish.

  5. 5

    Install

    Careful installation, a clean job site, and a final walkthrough so everything is just right.
